Our Story.

As a proud Nevadan, born and raised in Reno, Wild West Hardscapes is about so much more than building beautiful outdoor spaces. One of the biggest reasons I wanted to start this business was to work alongside my brother, because family has always meant everything to me.

Together, we want to build more than a business. We want to create a strong foundation for our children, build generational wealth, and leave behind a legacy they can be proud of.

Every project we take on is another step toward that dream, built with family, hard work, and heart.

    Common questions

    Are your estimates really free?

    Yes. Call, text or send the form and we'll set up a free project evaluation and estimate.

    Are you licensed and insured?

    Yes — Wild West Hardscapes LLC is licensed and insured, operating under Nevada State Contractor License #0096054.

    Why plan a project in winter?

    Booking during the winter means your design and prep are done ahead of the spring rush — and you can take advantage of our Winter Specials while spots last.

    What pavers do you install?

    We feature Cambridge Cobble pavers, available in a range of colors, and can talk through other options during your estimate.

    Can you do the whole backyard, not just one piece?

    Absolutely. We handle complete outdoor remodels — patios, walls, turf, putting greens, fire pits, water features, lighting and xeriscape — so one crew brings the whole vision together.

    How do I qualify for the free upgrade?

    Spend $13,000 or more on a new backyard installation or remodel to receive 800 sq. ft. of Cambridge Cobble pavers plus a free fire pit or 6 paver lights. Only 10 spots are available.
